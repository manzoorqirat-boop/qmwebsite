Who we are

One studio, many industries.

QMSofts is a software studio building focused, AI-powered tools for specific industries — schools, pharmacies, hotels, diagnostic labs, distributors and manufacturers. Rather than forcing every business into one generic system, we build a platform around each trade's real, daily workflow.

Every product shares the same modern foundation: cloud-hosted with nothing to install, multi-tenant so many businesses run safely side by side, and built with intelligence woven in — OCR capture, natural-language parsing, and quick-fill assistance that take the friction out of routine work.
